01 — Why it matters

Why should we recycle?

Packaging protects products, but after we use it, it can become waste. Recycling gives materials another life and helps reduce the amount of rubbish sent to landfill.

🌳

Save resources

Reuse materials instead of using only new raw materials.

🏙️

Less waste

Keep more useful materials out of landfills.

⚡

Save energy

Recycling can use less energy than making some products from raw materials.

🌍

Protect nature

Reduce pollution and help protect ecosystems.

02 — Materials

What packaging can we recycle?

🧴

Plastic

Some bottles, tubs and containers. Check the local recycling rules and plastic type.

📦

Paper & cardboard

Clean boxes, paper bags, newspapers and cereal boxes.

🫙

Glass

Glass bottles and jars. Empty and rinse them first.

🥫

Metal

Aluminium drink cans and steel food tins.

🧃

Drink cartons

Milk and juice cartons may be recyclable at special collection points because they contain several layers of material.

FOUR SIMPLE STEPS04 / 08
04 — Not everything

What may not be accepted?

  • Greasy or food-soiled cardboard
  • Some plastic bags and wrappers
  • Foam packaging
  • Multi-layer packets that local facilities cannot process

These items may need special collection or may not be recyclable in your area.

🚫📦
Tip: when in doubt, look for your city's recycling guide instead of putting unknown items in the recycling bin.
